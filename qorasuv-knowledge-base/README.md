## Requirements

- Tashkent city, Mirzo Ulug‘bek district, near Qorasuv.
- 2 or 3 rooms (room count does not necessarily mean bedrooms).
- Maximum advertised purchase price: **US$75,000**.
- Apartments and private courtyard houses are acceptable.
- Qorasuv block, landmark, maximum distance, preferred floor, and whether the budget includes renovation and transaction costs remain unspecified.

## Findings

Eight apartment leads advertise prices within the cap; two additional leads are old archive listings. No private courtyard house meeting all the stated criteria was established in this search. This is a search result, not proof that none exists.

Start by checking Q001, Q002 and Q003 because their indexed evidence is comparatively recent. Q004 offers a lower floor but needs renovation. Q005 is a 3-room option requiring renovation. All availability, prices, addresses and property details need seller confirmation.

This knowledge base preserves the available advertised details, including unknown fields, conflicting prices, older listings and exclusions. It does not claim exhaustive internet coverage. No seller has been contacted and no property has been inspected. Some direct pages failed to load; their records rely on indexed search results.

## Location errors and excluded house results

“Qorasuv / Карасу” is used for multiple places. Restrict searches to Tashkent city and Mirzo Ulug‘bek district; a Qorasuv city category alone is insufficient.

## Seller questions and viewing notes

For each lead record: availability; exact address or map pin; Qorasuv block; current asking price and currency; minimum negotiated price; agent fee; actual cadastral room count; area; renovation estimate; furniture included; floor and functioning lift; roof condition for top-floor apartments; water, heating and utility condition; residential registration status and documents; viewing date; decision.

Ask the seller: “Is the property still for sale? Please send its exact address, current price, room count according to the cadastral document, floor, renovation condition, and any agent commission. Our purchase-price limit is US$75,000.”
